Oil’s Geopolitical Reversal Drives a Broad Market Rally as AI and U.S. Growth Stay Firm

Summary

The central market story from August 3 through August 4, 2026, was a sharp reversal in the geopolitical risk premium attached to energy. President Donald Trump postponed planned military strikes on Iran and promoted renewed negotiations over the Strait of Hormuz, although Iranian officials disputed that direct U.S.-Iran talks were taking place. Oil prices fell roughly 5%, Treasury yields declined, and U.S. equities rallied, with the Dow closing at a record and the Nasdaq gaining more than 2%. Strong U.S. manufacturing data and renewed enthusiasm for large technology companies reinforced the risk-on move. Amazon crossed a $3 trillion market value, while Palantir reported rapid AI-related revenue growth and raised its outlook after the closing bell. In healthcare, reported merger discussions between AstraZeneca and Bristol Myers Squibb produced a sharply negative reaction in AstraZeneca shares. The outlook remains highly dependent on whether Middle East diplomacy produces verifiable improvements in commercial shipping through Hormuz and whether upcoming labor data confirm that U.S. economic growth remains resilient.
